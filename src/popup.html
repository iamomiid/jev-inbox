Jev Inbox Saved

Jev Inbox sends the sender, subject, a short snippet and date of unread emails in the list currently loaded to Jev (through Vercel AI Gateway or TypeSafe, depending on your key) to classify them. Your label names and descriptions are sent with each classification as criteria, so do not put sensitive information in them. Email bodies are never sent. Results are cached in this browser.
